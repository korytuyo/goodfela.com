Pizza Rush

Il Pipelino, Friday night. Orders land on the conveyor. You build the kitchen line that serves them. Get it wrong and the health inspector, the fire, or the local paper finds out first.

The loop

Read the order. Tap the stages the order requires — nothing more. Hit Enter to serve. Ten seconds, tops.

The rules of the line

Miss a required stage or add a forbidden one → disaster, −1 health star. Extra stages you weren't asked for cost 40 pts each. Three or more extras jam the line and the order goes cold.

Combo

Clean serves stack a multiplier up to ×3. Speed pays: every second left on the clock is points. Five stars is your health rating. Lose them all and you're closed.
